# Step 1 — Find problems

Researched 2 November 2026. Goal: a real, repeated pain point that a small digital
product can fix and that can be sold on Gumroad/Payhip and Instagram.

## Candidates

| Problem | Evidence | Can a simple product fix it? | Competition | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Freelancers chasing late invoices** | Freelancers spend ~20 days/year chasing late payments; 66% feel uncomfortable chasing and 57% fear losing the client; ~half of UK freelancers have considered quitting over late payment | Yes: the cause is mostly process (no due date, no late-fee terms, no reminder routine, chasing only when angry) | Big invoicing software exists, but it's subscription and overkill for solo freelancers | **Pick** |
| Instagram/WhatsApp sellers losing orders in DMs | Sellers "lose orders in chat history or manually copy them into Excel"; spreadsheets break at ~5 orders/day | Partly | Crowded: Pingsold, Orders App, Loopy and others | Runner-up (next product) |
| Small business data scattered across sheets/WhatsApp | Many anecdotal posts | Too broad for one product | Huge | No |

## The pick: "Paid On Time"

**Who:** solo freelancers (designers, writers, developers, VAs, social media managers).

**Pain:** they finish the work, then waste hours and feel awkward chasing money. They
wait until they're frustrated, send an angry email, and the relationship sours.

**Simple fix:** a routine. Clear terms before the job, a tracker that shows exactly
who is overdue today, and pre-written reminders that escalate politely on a schedule,
so chasing becomes a 2-minute copy-paste instead of an emotional event.
