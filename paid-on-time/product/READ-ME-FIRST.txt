PAID ON TIME: The Freelancer's Invoice-Chasing Kit
==================================================

Thanks for buying! Here's what's inside and where to start.

1. Paid-On-Time-Playbook.pdf   Read this first (10 minutes). The 3 rules, payment terms
                               to copy, the reminder ladder, every script, and what to
                               say to excuses.
2. Paid-On-Time-App.html       Double-click to open it in your browser. Add your details in
                               Settings, then add every unpaid invoice. Open it each
                               morning and send what it tells you.
                               Your data stays in that browser, so use "Export backup" weekly.
3. Paid-On-Time-Tracker.xlsx   Prefer spreadsheets? Use this in Excel or Google Sheets
                               instead of the app. The scripts are on the last tab.
