# Getting paid in South Africa from international buyers

Checked 2 November 2026. These services change their country lists often, so confirm in
each one's own help pages before you sign up.

## For selling the kit (a store that takes the card payment and pays you out)

| Option | Pays out to SA? | Why use it | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lemon Squeezy** | Yes: bank payouts to SA are on its official list, and PayPal payouts too | It's the "merchant of record", so it charges and pays VAT/sales tax for buyers in the EU, UK, US and elsewhere. That's a big headache removed | Fees are reported around 5% + 50c per sale. Bank payouts are sent in USD and converted |
| **Gumroad** | Its help page lists South Africa for bank payouts in ZAR. If bank payouts aren't available, it falls back to PayPal (about 2% fee) | Easiest to set up, and it also handles sales tax | Reported $100 minimum before a payout. Check the current country list before you rely on it |
| **Paystack (SA)** | Yes, settles in ZAR to your SA bank | Payment links and checkout on your own site. Takes international Visa, Mastercard and Amex | You handle tax yourself. International cards reported at about 3.1% + R1. USD payouts for SA businesses are unclear |

**Recommendation:** use **Lemon Squeezy** (or Gumroad) for the kit, because they deal with
foreign sales tax for you. Keep PayPal as a second payout route.

## For client work, custom jobs and direct payments

| Option | How you get the money | Cost (reported) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PayPal** | Withdraw to your SA bank through FNB's PayPal service (open to more banks now) | FNB charges up to about 1.5%, plus PayPal's own fees and currency conversion | Most international buyers already have it. Takes 0–5 business days |
| **Payoneer** | Gives you USD/EUR/GBP receiving details clients can pay by bank transfer, then you withdraw to your SA bank | Up to about 2% to convert to ZAR | Good for agencies and companies that want to pay an invoice by bank transfer |
| **Your bank's foreign currency account** (FNB, Standard Bank and others) | Clients pay by SWIFT straight to your bank | SWIFT and conversion fees, which vary by bank | Slower, but no middleman |
| **Wise** | Unclear for SA residents | — | Wise is still launching in SA. Don't rely on it for receiving USD yet |

## Put it in the app
In **Paid On Time → Settings → How clients pay you**, list two or three of these, for example:

```
PayPal: you@email.com
Bank transfer (USD): [your Payoneer receiving details]
Card: [your Paystack or Lemon Squeezy payment link]
```
Every reminder then includes them automatically.

## Tax and exchange control (not advice, just what to know)
- Income from foreign clients is taxable in South Africa if you're a SA tax resident. Keep
  records of every payout.
- When foreign money is converted into rand, your bank or the payout service will ask what
  it's for (for example "export of services" or "sale of digital goods"). That's normal.
- For anything large or unclear, speak to a SA accountant.
