## Description

**You did the work. Why are you still waiting to get paid?**

Freelancers lose weeks every year chasing late invoices. Most of us hate doing it, so we
put it off, then send an annoyed email when we finally snap. The client gets defensive.
It gets awkward. The money still doesn't arrive.

Late payment usually isn't the client being evil. It's a missing process: no clear due
date, the invoice sitting in the wrong inbox, and no reminder until it's already a problem.

**Paid On Time gives you the process.**

**What's inside**

- **The Paid On Time app.** Open it in any browser, with no account, no subscription and
  no internet needed. Add your invoices, and every morning it shows who to remind today
  and writes the email or WhatsApp message for you. Copy, send, done.
- **The invoice tracker spreadsheet** for Excel, Google Sheets and Numbers. It flags
  overdue invoices and tells you which reminder each one needs.
- **12 reminder scripts.** An email and a WhatsApp/DM version for each of the 6 steps:
  heads-up, due today, friendly nudge, firm reminder, final notice, pause work.
- **The 9-page Paid On Time Playbook.** A payment-terms clause to copy, the invoice
  checklist, the reminder ladder, and word-for-word replies to "it's with accounts",
  "cash is tight" and "we're waiting on our client".

**Who it's for:** designers, writers, developers, VAs, social media managers, video
editors, and anyone who invoices clients and is tired of chasing.

**Who it's not for:** agencies that already run invoicing software with automatic reminders.

**What people get out of it**
- Never forget to follow up on an invoice again
- Reminders that are polite, firm and never emotional
- Clear terms, so late fees are agreed instead of argued about
- A 2-minute daily habit instead of a monthly panic

*Your data stays on your device. The app doesn't send it anywhere.*
