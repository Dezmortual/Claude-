# Step 4 — Repeat forever

*Improve it. Scale it. Reach more people. Then do it all again.*

## The weekly loop (every Sunday, ~30 minutes)

1. **Measure** — fill one row in `weekly-scorecard.csv`.
2. **Listen** — read every DM answer and refund reason in `feedback-log.csv`. Tag each
   one: `bug`, `feature`, `objection` or `new-problem`.
3. **Decide one thing** using the table below. One change per week, so you know what worked.
4. **Ship it** — ask Claude to make the change, re-zip, and upload the new version to
   Gumroad. Buyers get updates free, so email them about it ("New in Paid On Time: …").

| What the numbers say | What to change |
|---|---|
| Few profile visits / low reach | The **hook**: new first slide, new reel opening. Test a different pain ("client ghosted me"). |
| Lots of visits, few "PAID" comments | The **offer**: clearer slide 6, show the app in a reel, add a testimonial. |
| Lots of DMs, few sales | The **price or the page**: test $9 vs $19, add screenshots and an FAQ to the listing. |
| Sales but refunds or complaints | The **product**: fix the top `bug` or `objection` tag first. |
| Good sales | **Scale**: raise to $24, post twice as often, pitch freelancer newsletters/communities, offer a 30% affiliate cut on Gumroad. |

## Do it all again — the next problems, already researched

Start step 1 for the next product as soon as Paid On Time has **10 sales or 4 weeks of data**.

| Next problem | Why | Signal to watch in your DMs |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram/WhatsApp sellers losing orders in DMs** | Real pain (orders lost in chat history, spreadsheets break at ~5 orders/day). Apps exist, so go simple: an order tracker template plus DM reply scripts | Buyers who say "I sell products, not services" |
| **Client onboarding chaos** | Freelancers who get paid late also lack contracts and proposals. Same audience, so an easy upsell | `new-problem` tags about scope creep or contracts |
| **Pricing anxiety** ("what do I charge?") | Constant question in freelancer communities | DMs asking "how much should I charge?" |

## Rules that keep it honest
- Never claim results you haven't seen. Use real testimonials only, with permission.
- Keep the 30-day refund. It costs little and removes the biggest objection.
- Any statistic in a post must link to its source (see `01-research.md`).
