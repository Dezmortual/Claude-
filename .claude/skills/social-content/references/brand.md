## What it is
"A services firm for local and small businesses: website, Google profile,
social posts, bookkeeping and customer inbox, from one brief with one
accountable lead." Based in Knysna, South Africa. Serving South Africa and
the United States.

The idea: a wright is a maker (shipwright, playwright). Manywright brings
many crafts under one name. Instead of an agency, a freelancer, a consultant
and a bookkeeper ("four briefs, four invoices, nobody joining the dots"), the
client briefs one firm and gets one finished result.

## Audience
Owners of local and small businesses in South Africa and the US who know
they should be doing their website, Google profile, social and books
properly but never get to it. The site describes them as: "Your Facebook page
last posted in spring. Instagram is mostly photos from opening week… there is
always something more urgent." They want to brief someone once, know the
price up front, and not manage four suppliers.

## Voice
Plain, direct, specific, calm, quietly confident. Short declarative
sentences. Concrete numbers and limits stated up front (prices, turnaround,
what's *not* included). Honest about scope ("Not a pen test." "No tax filing
or tax advice."). No hype, no exclamation marks, no jargon.

Sample from the site: "Every limit is written into your proposal before
anything starts."

Spelling: the site mixes US and UK ("colors", but "31 January 2027"). Use US
spelling and dollar prices by default; UK/SA spelling and rand for posts
aimed at South Africa.

## Never say
- **No testimonials, client logos, case studies, client results or
  numbers about clients.** The site says: "No borrowed logos, no
  testimonials." Until the owner adds approved proof below, there is none.
- No guarantees of rankings, followers, leads or revenue.
- Nothing that sounds like legal, medical or tax advice. Bookkeeping posts
  must not suggest tax filing or tax advice.
- Don't call the security check-up a "pen test" or "audit certification".
- Don't say paid ads are included in social plans (they aren't), or that
  social plans reply to comments/DMs (that's Customer inbox).
- Don't present "199 specialist roles" as 199 employees; it's a bench of
  specialist roles.
- Don't name the founder unless the owner supplies a name (Owner to confirm).
- Hype words: unlock, elevate, supercharge, game-changer, skyrocket,
  10x, revolutionize, cutting-edge, "in today's fast-paced world".

## Visual style
- Look: technical drawing / blueprint. Numbered "Sheets" ("Sheet 01 / 09"),
  coordinates ("X 018.5 Y 064.0"), figure captions ("Fig. 2 —"), grid
  lines, the M logo drawn as "4 pieces".
- Colors: navy-900 `#0f1b2d` (primary dark), navy-700 `#1e2f4a`,
  navy-600 `#2a3b57`, off-white `#f4f6fa`, amber-500 `#f5a524` (accent),
  sky-400 `#4cc9f0`, coral-400 `#ff6b5e`, slate-500 `#5b6779`, line-200 `#d5dbe5`.
- Fonts: Archivo (headings/body), IBM Plex Mono (labels, numbers, captions).
- Graphic briefs: navy grid background, off-white Archivo headline, one
  amber highlight, a mono-font label such as "SHEET 03 · GET FOUND LOCALLY".
  No stock photos of handshakes or laptops.
