Where to Find
Problems.

Listen for complaints. They’re everywhere.
Look here
Your own frustrations.
What annoyed you this week?
Someone else has the same problem.
Look here
Reddit and forums.
Search “I hate when” or “is there a tool for”
plus your niche.
Look here
Bad reviews.
1–3 star reviews of popular products
tell you exactly what’s missing.
Look here
Questions you get asked.
If friends keep asking you,
strangers will pay for the answer.

Build It
Small.

The smallest thing that fixes the problem.
Easiest
A template.
Spreadsheet, Notion or Canva.
You can build one in a day.
Easy
A guide.
Step-by-step PDF with checklists
and copy-paste scripts.
Medium
A simple tool.
A one-page app. Can’t code?
Use AI to help you build it.
Start today
A service.
Do it for clients by hand first.
Turn it into a product later.
Done beats perfect. Ship in a week.

Sell It
Online.

Package it. Price it. Share it.
01
Name the result.
“Paid On Time”, not “Invoice Template v2”.
People buy outcomes.
02
Price it simply.
$9–$29 for a first digital product.
A launch discount for early buyers.
03
Use a ready store.
Gumroad or Payhip. Free to start,
and they handle the payments.
04
Post the problem.
Not the product. Show the fix,
then say “comment for the link”.
Then improve it, and repeat.
