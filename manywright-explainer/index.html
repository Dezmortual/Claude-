"Lines to manage" counts every pair among you and your suppliers who may need to talk to each other: n(n+1)/2 for n suppliers. Four suppliers make 10 lines. Six make 21. Each one is a place where messages get lost.

Sheet 03The idea

One brief. One firm. The right specialists behind it.

A wright is a maker. A shipwright builds ships; a playwright builds plays. Manywright brings many crafts under one name. You describe the work once, to the founder, who stays your single point of contact. Behind that lead sits a bench of 199 specialist roles in 13 disciplines, called in only when your work needs them.
