5 things customers check before they call you.

They search, glance at your Google profile for a few seconds, and either tap Call or move on to the next business.

01

Your hours are right.

Nothing loses a customer faster than a closed door. If Google says you're open and you're not, they won't give you a second chance.

FIX IT IN 5 MINUTES
  1. Search your business name on Google while signed in, and tap Edit profile.
  2. Check your opening hours match reality.
  3. Add special hours for public holidays before they arrive.

02

Your photos are recent.

Photos are how customers judge you before they call. Old or missing photos make a business look closed or careless.

FIX IT IN 5 MINUTES
  1. Add a clear photo of your shopfront or sign.
  2. Add 3–5 photos of your actual work.
  3. Add one of you or your team. People buy from people.

03

Every review has a reply.

Customers read your replies as much as the reviews. A calm reply to a bad review is really written for the next customer.

FIX IT IN 5 MINUTES
  1. Thank happy customers by name.
  2. For bad reviews: stay calm, own what went wrong, offer to fix it.
  3. Reply within a few days, every time.

04

Your site works on a phone.

Most local searches happen on a phone. If people have to pinch and zoom, or wait for the page to load, they leave.

FIX IT IN 5 MINUTES
  1. Open your website on your own phone.
  2. Try to find your phone number in under 5 seconds.
  3. If you can't, your customers can't either.

05

One tap to call you.

A customer who wants to call shouldn't have to copy and paste a number. Every extra step loses some of them.

FIX IT IN 5 MINUTES
  1. Check your phone number is on your Google profile.
  2. Put a tap-to-call button at the top of every page of your site.
  3. Test it on your phone. Tap it. Does it ring?
