## Blog article for manywright.com (helps people find you on Google)

**Title:** 5 things customers check on Google before they call a local business

**Meta description:** Before a customer calls, they check your Google profile. Here are the 5 things that decide whether they call you or the next business, and how to fix each one.

> Most customers decide whether to call you before they ever visit your website. They search, look at your Google profile for a few seconds, and either tap "Call" or move on to the next result. Here's what they're looking at.
>
> **1. Your hours are right.** Nothing loses a customer faster than turning up to a closed door. Check your Google hours match reality, and add special hours for public holidays.
>
> **2. Your photos are recent.** Photos tell customers you're open and active. Add a clear shopfront photo, a few of your work, and one of your team. Replace anything that's years old.
>
> **3. Every review has a reply.** Customers read your replies as much as the reviews. Thank people for good reviews by name. For bad ones, reply calmly, own what went wrong, and offer to fix it. That reply is really for the next customer reading it.
>
> **4. Your website works on a phone.** Most local searches happen on a phone. If customers have to pinch and zoom, or the page takes ages to load, they leave. Open your site on your own phone and try to find your phone number in under 5 seconds.
>
> **5. One tap to call you.** Your phone number should be one tap away, on your Google profile and on every page of your site.
>
> **Not sure how you score?** Manywright does a free "Found on Google" check: a one-page review of your Google profile and your website on a phone, sent by email within 3 business days. [Ask for your free check →](https://manywright.com)
