> comparable apps: 429 App Store reviews of 7 AI signal and forecast apps, read from Apple's official
> customer-reviews RSS feed, plus 4 Hacker News comments from the Algolia API. So this is evidence about the
> category, not about BeCoin. Themes are matched by keyword, so some quotes are filed under the wrong theme
> (for example "Scam" under billing). "Teams and sharing" is all misfires on the word "share" and is ignored
