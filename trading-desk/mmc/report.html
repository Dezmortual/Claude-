The setups

  • 2B false breakout + pokebar. The pokebar is the first bar to close above a recent swing high (no more than one ATR above it). Then a bearish candle closes below the pokebar's low: sell. Stop at the second top, target 2R. Buys mirror it.
  • 2B with the daily trend. The same, but only selling false highs when the daily close is under its 50-day EMA, and only buying false lows above it. The notes call this the best way to trade them.
  • RSI divergence, second swing. A higher swing high with a lower RSI(14): sell at the close that confirms the swing. Stop above it, target 2R.
  • MMC footprint + leverage. A trendline touched twice, then a close through the previous high starts the trade. While it is in profit, each new high adds a unit (up to five). One stop trails under each new swing low, with no target.

The notes give no profit targets for the first three, so 2R is used. A swing point is a high or low above or below the three bars on each side.

Real edge, eaten by costs

RSI divergence on 15-minute charts wins about a third of its trades at 2R. Before costs that is a real edge: risking 1% a trade would have grown an account about 137 times. Its stops are only 11 pips on average, so a 1–3 pip spread costs about a fifth of the risk on every trade, and the same trades wipe the account out.

What the tests show

  • Short timeframes lose most. Every setup loses heavily on 15-minute charts, where stops are about 11–20 pips and the spread is a big share of each one.
  • 4-hour charts come closest. Stops are 45–90 pips, so costs matter less, but the edge left over is close to zero.
  • The daily-trend filter helps the 2B a little, from −0.9% to +2.7% per trade on 4h, but not enough to be distinguishable from chance.
  • Leverage doesn't rescue the footprint. Most campaigns never get to add a unit (about 2 in 3 stay at one), and three in four lose overall.

All runs

"Per trade" is profit or loss as a % of the amount risked. "Account" is the change in balance over 8.7 years risking 1% per trade, compounded. For the footprint, each added unit counts as a trade. Costs per round trip: EUR/USD 1.0 pip, USD/JPY 1.2, AUD/USD 1.3, GBP/USD 1.5, USD/CHF 1.6, GBP/JPY 2.8.

What isn't covered
  • The notes also use hand-drawn support and resistance zones and reversal candlesticks as extra confirmation. Those depend on judgement and aren't coded here.
  • The notes contradict themselves on divergence ("enter on the 2nd touch", then "always use the 3rd touch"). The second touch is tested.
  • The 30-minute/5-minute "chord" pairing isn't modelled. Each timeframe is tested on its own.
  • Prices are HistData 1-minute bid quotes, with no commission, swaps or news filter.
