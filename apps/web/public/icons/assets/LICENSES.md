# Where the asset logos come from

| files | source | licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| coin logos (`bitcoin.svg` … `injective.svg`) | [@web3icons/core](https://github.com/0xa3k5/web3icons) 4.0.58, "background" variant | MIT |
| `aapl.svg`, `nvda.svg`, `tsla.svg`, `googl.svg` | glyphs and brand colours from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) 16.35.0, on a circle | CC0-1.0 |
| `flags/*.svg` | [flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons) 7.6.0, square | MIT |
| `gold.svg`, `silver.svg`, `wti.svg` | drawn for FXDEZ | ours |

The logos stay their owners' trademarks; they're shown only to identify the asset. Ethena, Microsoft and Amazon
have no openly licensed logo in these sets, so they keep the letter mark.
